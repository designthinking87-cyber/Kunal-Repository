## Review

- 155 authored actor pose keys across two fighters; regenerated geometry for each export frame.
- 13 camera beats, each no longer than 2.5 seconds, with short shutter handoffs.
- Contact frames, landing poses, title placement and blade crossing were inspected from actual Chromium screenshots at 1080p.
- HyperFrames lint and browser/runtime/layout checks were run. Canvas internals are beyond DOM layout auditing. A canvas static-sweep warning does not validate or invalidate character action; screenshots and output-frame differences are checked separately.
- Software-WebGL performance warnings are environmental, not application exceptions.
- The animation is an independent stylised vector-pose production, not the source-derived replacement technique mentioned in historical skill notes.
- Full-speed human visual playback and a listening audition were not available in the tool interface. Do not treat sampled frame inspection or audio measurements as a complete performance/listening review.
- Sound is a new synthesized effects mix; music and voices are absent. Final metadata and audio measurements are saved in verification.json.
