# Storm / Edge
## Style Prompt
Original 2D illustrated combat on an abandoned rain-soaked rooftop above a midnight harbour. Flat graphic silhouettes, ink contours, selective warm edge light and cold atmospheric depth. Preserve the supplied bald peach-headed character, large expressive eyes, white tank, black gloves and stars-and-stripes shorts. New faceless indigo challenger with a red sash. No 3D rendering. Fast authored poses, tight contact timing, planted support, weapon grips and expressive recoil. No source footage or soundtrack in the finished animation.
## Colors
- #070e1b midnight atmosphere
- #143349 rooftop and skyline
- #75dceb cold light and blade
- #ffba68 warm rim / impact
- #f3dfcd skin / #f3eee6 vest
## Typography
Arial Black for the small final film title; Arial for production notes. No dialogue, subtitles or UI in the fight.
## What NOT to Do
No glossy 3D, no headwear on protagonist, no constant camera shaking, no endless idle bouncing, no speech or background music. Effects follow contact and movement rather than cover it.
