# The rare human birth — PixiJS

## Style Prompt
A luminous sculptural transformation on absolute black: a vast orbital archive of life resolves into one extraordinary human birth. Gold line-drawn fish, birds, insects, leaves and mammals travel through a field of ivory points. The field compresses into a soul seed, then expands into a human figure formed from light. Ceremonial Devanagari typography balances the sculpture on the left. The ending breathes; the human form is the payoff.

## Colors

- #f5efdf warm ivory particles and main lettering

## Typography
Tiro Devanagari Hindi Regular, locally embedded, for its calligraphic devotional register. Large opening numerals and large final headline; all Hindi receives proper shaping and generous line boxes. No Latin filler or microtext.

## Narrative and motion
0–3.2s: scale and multiplicity — 84 lakh, orbital line artwork of different forms of life, moving luminous seed.
3.2–5.8s: continuous contraction / transformation — paths accelerate into a central seed; the particles assemble into a standing human figure. A clear causal transition, no jump cut or black gap.
5.8–10s: meaning and stillness — मानव जन्म, rare and precious; halo line, luminous chest, quiet breathing particles. A soft long sound tail ends at 10s.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not tint the background; pure black is explicitly requested.
- No stock photos, human footage, logos, UI frames or colored panels.
- No literal 8.4-million-object simulation or claim of scientific evolutionary ancestry. This is symbolic spiritual imagery.
- No full-frame flash, strobing, constant zooming or huge lens flare.
- No synthetic narration substituting for the user's own voice. Original restrained sound design only.
