# Enduring light — cinematic Three.js sequence
## Style Prompt
A solitary, faceless traveller on an immense weathered stone ring suspended over midnight water. A small unwavering golden companion light provides the emotional anchor. Long camera glides reveal scale, then return to stillness. A crisp ivory Hindi question closes over a retained water reflection. Procedural geometry and water, seeded haze and particles; no stock footage or deity imagery.
## Colors
- #07131F midnight atmosphere
- #111F2C ocean
- #38424A weathered charcoal stone
- #FFF1D4 ivory typography
- #E8BD65 companion gold
## Typography
Bundled Noto Sans Devanagari regular and bold, correct shaping. Reveal labels at 54px; closing question 86px with generous safe margins.
## Motion
Close orb opening, graceful backward glide, camera rise to enormous ring from 6–10s, soft transition back to companion at 10s, quiet pause, dark dissolve at 13s. Deterministic scene evaluation at absolute seconds. No camera shake or flashing.
## What NOT to Do
No deity likeness, no busy ornamental graphics, no unreadable microtext, no random animation, no crushed stone detail, no saturated orange atmosphere.
