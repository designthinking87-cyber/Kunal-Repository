# Enduring companion — Motion Canvas
## Style Prompt
Luminous vector silhouettes and a single continuous golden stroke against a vast midnight field. The human figure stays small, ivory, and faceless. Soft overlapping landscape silhouettes and restrained parallax provide depth. Smoke ribbons suggest attention turning away, while the companion light remains present. A large change of scale reveals a three-node circular path. Correctly shaped Hindi and a final question give the story a clear destination.
## Colors
- #07131F midnight canvas
- #102638 / #173449 charcoal-blue layered silhouettes
- #FFF1D4 warm ivory silhouette and labels
- #E8BD65 restrained companion gold
- #718797 soft smoky ribbons, used transparently
## Typography
Bundled Noto Sans Devanagari bold. Nodes ~66px at full view; closing question 78–82px. No extra headings, badges, microtext, or Latin placeholders.
## Motion
A controlled opening pullback and slow parallax. Curves draw with path progress. Ribbon movement is restrained. One decisive camera withdrawal reveals the cycle. Nodes and connecting arcs appear sequentially; one gold pulse follows. Separate timing variants serve standalone and original narration.
## Avoid
No flashes, shake, particle explosions, busy ornamental patterns, paid assets, Remotion, or overwriting the original project.
