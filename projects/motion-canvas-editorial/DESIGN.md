# Karma — complete editorial Short
## Visual identity
Midnight #07131F, charcoal #102638, warm ivory #FFF1D4, restrained gold #E8BD65. Original warm footage stays natural. Graphics use luminous vector silhouettes, fine consistent gold lines, and soft parallax. Gold accents communicate presence and attention, not decoration.
## Typography
Bundled Noto Sans Devanagari bold and regular, correctly shaped whole words. Hook has large editorial hierarchy (140–164px). Captions are 70–112px, arranged as meaningful phrases over the lower chest with a soft contrast scrim. No subtitle boxes or Latin placeholders.
## Narrative
0–3s: original narration over full-screen editorial question; protective gold circle becomes a question mark. At 3s the circle reveals the face. Subsequent treatment alternates talking head, full-screen compassion typography, companion graphic, human-birth diagram, distraction ribbon overlay, cycle explanation, and concluding talking-head question.
## Motion
Masked phrase entrances, shape-matched transitions, slight push-in for “छोड़ते नहीं हैं,” steady companion light, stepwise diagrams. Pacing follows the transcript, not a fixed effects timer. Let the important captions settle.
## Avoid
No face in the first three seconds. No narration edits, image warping, microtext, generic caption boxes, emojis, cursors, particle explosions, repeated zooms or flashes. Motion Canvas is the sole animation/composition system; FFmpeg only encodes and restores original audio.
